( 01 — Overview )

A clinic that doesn't exist, built to be tested.

Smile Dental is a dental clinic in Pune that I made up, so I could build the whole system and test it from the first message to the booked appointment.

It has a website with treatment pages, a visit planner for patients travelling in from abroad, and Riya, who answers chats on the site. Every patient, phone number and booking on this page is sample data.

( 02 — The problem )

Enquiries arrive when nobody is free to answer.

A small clinic's front desk is busy with the patients in front of it. Messages that come in during a procedure, at lunch or after closing have to wait.

Some of them are answered late and some are forgotten. A patient who hears nothing back may simply call the next clinic on the list.

( 03 — What I set up )

Five parts, each with one job.

They pass the enquiry along, so nobody has to copy it from one place to another.

Website
Explains each treatment in plain words, with a planner for patients travelling in.
Riya
Answers chats on the website and takes the patient's name, phone and preferred time.
Enquiry list
A Google Sheet that gets one new row for every patient, filled in automatically.
Inbox alerts
An email to the clinic for every new enquiry.
Appointment diary
When the patient confirms a time, the appointment goes into the clinic's Google Calendar.

( 04 — One enquiry )

From a Hinglish message to a call-back, without anyone typing.

The same four steps as on the home page, with the real screenshots, larger.

  1. 01

    Ask

    A customer messages your website at any hour, in their own words.

  2. 02

    Answer

    Riya replies and takes their name, number and preferred time.

  3. 03

    Record

    It lands in your enquiry list, your inbox and your calendar. Nothing to copy.

  4. 04

    Confirm

    Your team calls back to confirm, with the details already in front of them.

( 05 — What the clinic gets )

Found, trusted, and answered, even after hours.

  • Every enquiry written down in one list, with the patient's name, phone number and preferred time.
  • An email for each one, so the front desk sees it without opening anything else.
  • The appointment in the diary once the patient confirms a time.
  • A person always in control: Riya takes the details, and your team calls the patient to confirm.
