( Questions )

Do you only work with clinics?

No. I started with clinics, so the clinic extras are ready, but the same setup works for any business that gets enquiries.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a ranking. I fix what Google looks at (your listing, reviews, details and website) and show you every month what changed.

Will Riya replace my receptionist?

No. She covers the moments your team can't: after hours, lunch and busy times. Your team still calls back and confirms.

Is my customers' data safe?

Requests are saved in your own Google Sheet and inbox. To reply, messages pass through the AI service and the automation server I run. Riya never gives a diagnosis or fees.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After the free report you get a fixed price in writing, before any work starts. Half is paid only at go-live.

How long does it take?

Most websites and Riya go live in about two weeks once I have your details.

Which languages does Riya reply in?

English, Hindi and Hinglish, matching the customer.

Who looks after it once it's live?

I do, personally. Message me on WhatsApp.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. You keep using WhatsApp, Gmail and Google Sheets the way you already do. I handle the rest.
